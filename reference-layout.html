HELP & ENQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about product enquiries, technical references and requesting a quotation from UTDSS.

What types of products can I enquire about?

UTDSS welcomes enquiries for marine and vessel supplies, industrial equipment and consumables, energy and offshore requirements, institutional procurement items and selected aviation-support products. Share your specific requirement so the team can assess whether sourcing support is possible.

Can UTDSS help with a difficult-to-source or discontinued item?

You can send the product details for review, even if the item is uncommon or difficult to identify. Include any manufacturer details, model, part numbers, photographs or specifications available. UTDSS will assess the request but cannot guarantee a match, replacement or supply.

What information should I provide to request a quotation?

Please include your contact name, organisation, reply-to email, product description and quantity. Manufacturer, model, NSN, MPN, technical specifications, required delivery location and preferred date are particularly helpful when available.

What are NSN and MPN, and do I need both?

NSN means National Stock Number, a standardised stock identification reference used in certain procurement systems. MPN means Manufacturer Part Number, which identifies a manufacturer's product. You do not need both, but providing a known reference alongside the product description helps reduce ambiguity. If you have neither, provide a detailed specification or model instead.

How do I send my quotation enquiry?

Use the Smart Sourcing Desk for a detailed multi-item enquiry or the quick quotation form for a simpler request. Both prepare an email addressed to Utdss@singnet.com. Your own email application opens the draft; review it, add any attachments and press Send. The website does not send the email automatically.

Can I attach product photographs, drawings or specifications?

Yes. Supporting documents can help the team identify your requirement. Files selected in the website form are not uploaded or automatically attached to the prepared email. Attach them yourself in Outlook or your email application before sending.

Are prices, stock levels and delivery times shown on the website confirmed?

No. The website presents sourcing categories and enquiry guidance, not a live stock catalogue or confirmed pricing. Availability, suitability, lead times, delivery arrangements and commercial terms must be reviewed and confirmed for each request.

How soon will UTDSS respond to an enquiry?

Timing varies according to product complexity, the completeness of your specifications and supplier responses. UTDSS does not advertise a guaranteed quotation turnaround time. Providing accurate part details and quantities can help the team assess your request.

Can I request delivery outside Singapore?

Include the delivery country, city or port, preferred date and any relevant delivery requirements. The team can review the request and confirm whether suitable arrangements are available; international delivery is not guaranteed for every item or destination.

Can I save an unfinished sourcing enquiry?

The Smart Sourcing Desk provides a Save Draft option that stores the entered information locally in your browser on that device. It is not submitted to UTDSS. Avoid saving sensitive details on shared computers, and restore or clear your draft using the controls on that page.

How to Request Supplies from UTDSS

Our enquiry process is designed for purchasers who have a complete part-number list and for customers who only know the product they need. Providing accurate specifications helps our team review your request efficiently.

01 / IDENTIFY

Describe your requirement

Start with the item name, intended application and quantity. For technical products, include an NSN, manufacturer part number (MPN), brand, dimensions or other specifications where available.

02 / SUBMIT

Choose your enquiry channel

Complete the item-by-item enquiry form and open a prepared email in your email application (Outlook supported). Include the delivery location and required date in the remarks.

03 / REVIEW

Receive individual confirmation

Our team reviews the specifications and sourcing feasibility before confirming pricing, availability, certifications or lead times. No catalogue listing should be taken as a guaranteed stock commitment.
