Energy sector requirements

For energy and offshore facilities, provide the operating environment, relevant ratings or dimensions, quantities and approved brand or part numbers. Alternative products should never be treated as interchangeable without specification review.

For a quotation, include Description, NSN, MPN / Part No., Quantity and Remarks. Our team can review incomplete part-number information where sufficient descriptive specifications are provided.
